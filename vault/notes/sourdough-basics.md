A working sourdough loaf comes down to three variables: hydration, fermentation
time, and oven heat. I keep a 100% hydration starter and feed it 1:1:1 the night
before baking.

Rough method: autolyse the flour and water for 30 minutes, add the levain and
salt, then do four sets of stretch-and-folds spaced 30 minutes apart. Bulk ferment
until the dough is jiggly and domed, shape, cold-proof overnight, then bake in a
preheated dutch oven at 250C with the lid on for 20 minutes and off for 20.

The biggest mistake I made early on was under-fermenting. A dense crumb almost
always means the bulk ferment was cut short.
