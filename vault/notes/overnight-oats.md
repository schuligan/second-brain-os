tags:

A reliable overnight oats ratio I keep coming back to: 1/2 cup rolled oats, 1/2 cup
milk, 1/4 cup yogurt, 1 tablespoon chia seeds, a pinch of salt. Stir, refrigerate
overnight, top with fruit in the morning.

The chia seeds are what make the texture work — they thicken the milk into a
pudding-like consistency so the oats do not go gluey. This is my default weekday
breakfast because it takes two minutes the night before and zero in the morning.
