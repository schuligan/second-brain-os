My attempt at a focus routine, loosely borrowed from Cal Newport's framing of
deep work. Two 90-minute blocks before lunch, phone in another room, a single
tab open. Everything shallow — email, messages, errands — goes into the afternoon.

The hard part is not the focus itself, it is protecting the calendar so the blocks
survive contact with other people's requests. I treat the morning blocks as
already-booked meetings with myself.

Related reading lives in [[atomic-habits-notes]] — the cue/routine/reward loop is
basically the scaffolding that makes a routine like this stick.
