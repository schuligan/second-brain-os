Weekend project: build a self-watering balcony planter from a couple of stacked
storage bins. Bottom bin holds the water reservoir, top bin holds soil with a
wicking column running down into the reservoir.

Roadmap:

- Cut a wicking hole and fit a perforated cup as the wick.
- Drill an overflow hole so heavy rain cannot drown the roots.
- Plant cherry tomatoes and basil first — both are forgiving and I cook with both.

This ties back to [[overnight-oats]] only in the sense that I want fresh fruit on
top in summer, but the real motivation is having herbs on hand for cooking.
