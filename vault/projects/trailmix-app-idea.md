A side project idea: a lightweight hiking companion app called Trailmix. Core
loop is dead simple — pick a trail, download the map for offline use, and get
turn-by-turn waypoint nudges without a data connection.

Milestones I am thinking about:

- M1: import a GPX file and render the route on an offline map.
- M2: waypoint proximity alerts (buzz when you drift off-route).
- M3: a packing checklist that adapts to trail length and weather.

The whole thing should ship as a thin end-to-end slice first, per my notes in
[[the-pragmatic-programmer]]. No backend until M2 forces one.
