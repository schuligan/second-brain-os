Notes from *The Pragmatic Programmer*. The DRY principle (Don't Repeat Yourself)
is the one I cite most: every piece of knowledge should have a single, authoritative
representation in a system.

Other ideas worth keeping: tracer bullets over big up-front design, prototype to
learn then throw it away, and "good-enough software" as a deliberate engineering
choice rather than an excuse.

Useful for [[trailmix-app-idea]] — I want to build that one tracer-bullet style,
end to end thin slice first, instead of polishing one layer in isolation.
